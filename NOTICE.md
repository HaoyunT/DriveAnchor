# Review Access and Attribution

This copy is provided for peer review. No additional open-source license is
granted by this packaging step. Existing copyright and third-party license terms
continue to apply. Identifying institutional names belonging to the submitting
authors are replaced by "Anonymous Rights Holder" for double-blind review;
this does not transfer ownership or change the scope of any permission.

Third-party project names and their attribution are not author identities and
are retained. Data and maps must be obtained through their original providers.
